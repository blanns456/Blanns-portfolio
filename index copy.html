About Me

I'm Blanns Lapening, a web developer with 4 years of experience building modern, responsive, and user-friendly websites and web applications.

I specialize in WordPress and Elementor, along with front-end and full-stack development using Laravel, Angular, React, and Vue. I also work on Figma-to-WordPress implementation, landing pages, website redesigns, responsive improvements, troubleshooting, and ongoing website maintenance.

My goal is simple: turn ideas and designs into websites that look polished, work smoothly, and provide a great experience across devices.
